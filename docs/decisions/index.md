# Decisions

Every design decision has an ID, quoted in the code as a `DECISIONE:`
comment. _Origin_ says whether the choice is the team's, a kit default kept
and justified, or technical. The Italian source of truth is
`TRACCIAMENTO_MODIFICHE.md`.

## Model and data (Tier 1)

| ID                          | Decision                                                         | Rationale                                                                                                                                                             | Origin | Where                                     |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| <span id="d-01">D-01</span> | Threshold 0.30 for standard users (was 0.35)                     | A missed failure costs about 10× an unnecessary inspection (declared hypothesis). 0.20 would give 230 unnecessary inspections out of 720 and saturate the human queue | Team   | `utils_io.SOGLIE_PER_CRITICITA`           |
| <span id="d-02">D-02</span> | Threshold 0.20 for high and critical users                       | HIC only protects what crosses the threshold: a missed failure on a hospital never enters the queue. FN on critical/high 3/6 → 1/1                                    | Team   | `utils_io.SOGLIE_PER_CRITICITA`           |
| <span id="d-03">D-03</span> | `area_geografica` out of the model, kept for monitoring and stop | It acts as a proxy for label bias. Without area AUC 0.868 (from 0.862), recall gap 0.41 → 0.22                                                                        | Team   | `utils_io.prepara_feature`                |
| <span id="d-04">D-04</span> | Hypothesis: failures under-reported in the South                 | South and Islands have almost identical profiles but recorded failures 0.24 vs 0.45; only the South is badly calibrated                                               | Team   | [Dataset](../architecture/dataset.md)     |
| <span id="d-05">D-05</span> | No threshold increase in the South                               | South "false positives" may be real unrecorded failures; raising the threshold would amplify the bias (South recall 0.88 → 0.65)                                      | Team   | —                                         |
| <span id="d-06">D-06</span> | Low recall in North/Centre declared as a limit                   | Missed failures have no sensor signal (vibration 2.8 vs 4.6); calibration is correct, so it is not label bias                                                         | Team   | [Model card](../compliance/model-card.md) |
| <span id="d-07">D-07</span> | Confidence = `max(p, 1-p)`                                       | Kit default, simple and explainable. ## Human oversight (Tier 2)

| ID                          | Decision                                                                                                                                                               | Rationale                                                                                                       | Origin          | Where                          |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------ |
| <span id="d-08">D-08</span> | Routing: HIC for critical users or load reduction on high users; HITL if p ≥ 0.60, confidence < 0.80, alerted area or non-light action; HOTL only for low-risk routine | Absolute human veto where errors are irreversible; the AI acts alone only where errors are cheap and reversible | Kit + team      | `OversightManager.route`       |
| <span id="d-09">D-09</span> | Rule-based action: ≥ 0.85 reduce load (non-standard only) or urgent inspection; ≥ 0.60 maintenance; ≥ 0.10 routine                                                     | The kit always proposed "maintenance": the action must grow with risk                                           | Technical       | `proponi_azione`               |

| <span id="d-11">D-11</span> | Justification ≥ 15 chars mandatory; duplicates blocked; < 30 chars measured                                                                                            | An empty, "ok" or copied justification is not a judgement (T2, A4)                                              | Kit + technical | `revisiona`                    |
| <span id="d-12">D-12</span> | 30-minute SLA, then ESCALATION; manual escalation too                                                                                                                  | An unreviewed decision is never executed silently                                                               | Kit + technical | `controlla_sla`                |
| <span id="d-13">D-13</span> | Stop at three granularities plus combinations (e.g. HV lines in the South), including the existing queue                                                               | "Stop" also applies to what is already queued; no review under stop (T3)                                        | Technical       | `attiva_stop`, `_match_ambito` |
